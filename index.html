Give Protocol Foundation

The rails for global giving shouldn't decide who gets to receive it.

The global charitable sector moves hundreds of billions of dollars each year, yet the underlying infrastructure has not kept pace. Traditional funding flows remain fragmented by high cross-border fees, slow settlement times, and access barriers that leave the organizations closest to their communities on the periphery. At the same time, the volunteer labor and human dedication that sustain local efforts are rarely recognized alongside financial capital.

We build and support open-source, compliant digital tools designed to expand access, ensure transparent stewardship, and restore direct agency to communities and donors worldwide.

Friction costs are more than an operational expense—they are an access barrier.

When 4–12% of a cross-border contribution is absorbed by transfer fees, when funds take days or weeks to settle, or when community-level organizations lack access to traditional correspondent banking, the entire sector is constrained. These structural inefficiencies inadvertently dictate which causes receive resources and which communities can participate.

We believe that public giving should be as open, direct, and verifiable as the modern web. The Give Protocol Foundation stewards open public goods that reduce operational friction, ensure rigorous accountability, and connect participants directly to ground-level impact.

The Portfolio

Independent open tools, one shared commitment to public stewardship.

Each initiative addresses a specific gap in the philanthropic lifecycle — from how capital moves, to how impact is accounted for, to how human contribution is recognized and supported.

Give Protocol

Flagship · Testnet
Direct, compliant cross-border philanthropic rails

Low-cost, traceable transfer infrastructure that moves resources globally in minutes. Give Protocol pairs the velocity of open networks with established legal standards — incorporating verifiable 501(c)(3) status and international equivalency frameworks — ensuring donors can support legitimate, front-line initiatives directly, including those historically excluded by legacy banking barriers.

Pacioli

In Active Development
Open-source, verifiable financial accounting

Enterprise-grade, double-entry accounting software designed for the multi-chain and multi-currency era. Pacioli provides civil society organizations, treasuries, and nonprofits with self-hostable, transparent financial tooling — ensuring organizational stewardship is provable, auditable, and grounded in standard accounting discipline rather than retrospective reporting.

Volo Index

Early Stage
Recognizing volunteer leadership and community capacity

A diagnostic and development framework measuring volunteer-management and operational competency. Built on the belief that a healthy civil society runs on human dedication as much as capital, Volo Index provides organizations with the tools to cultivate, assess, and honor the vital sweat equity that powers community resilience.

How We Build: Foundation Principles

A measured, public-good approach to technology.

We pursue new tools only when they meet clear standards of sector utility and long-term viability. We favor measured, compliance-first progress over rapid expansion, working to build trust across both traditional institutions and open-source communities.

Public Good First

We focus strictly on structural infrastructure gaps where traditional markets have left communities underserved, building utilities that belong to the sector rather than a single entity.

Compliance & Trust by Design

Innovation is most powerful when it works constructively within regulatory and legal frameworks. We build with established accounting standards, verifiable verification, and institutional rigor from day one.

Sustainable Stewardship

We design systems with clear operational pathways that can cover their own maintenance without compromising our nonprofit mission or imposing tollbooths on the communities we serve.
